How to Convert Urdu or English Image to Excel

1. Choose an image.
2. Select Urdu + English for mixed documents, or choose one language for a single-language document.
3. Tap Extract Table / Text and wait for OCR to finish.
4. Check and, if necessary, correct cells directly in the detected table.
5. Download Excel (.xlsx) or CSV.

FAQ

Does it support Urdu?
Yes. This version loads the Urdu OCR model and can process Urdu + English together.

Will every handwritten Urdu character always be perfect?
No. Handwriting, blur, unusual fonts and low-resolution photos can cause OCR errors. Review important data before using it.

Is my image uploaded to a server?
The OCR is performed in your browser using Tesseract.js. Your image is not intentionally sent to a NiaziTools server.
