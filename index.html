Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert an image to Excel for free?

Yes. NiaziTools AI provides browser-based image-to-Excel conversion without requiring a paid account.

Does it support Urdu?

Yes. Select Urdu + English or Urdu from the language menu. OCR accuracy depends on image quality and text type.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and can be used on Android phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Is OCR 100% accurate?

No OCR system can guarantee 100% accuracy. Blurry images, handwriting, unusual fonts, shadows and complex tables can reduce accuracy. Always review important data.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

The OCR processing in this page is performed in your browser. The OCR language data and JavaScript libraries are loaded from their public CDNs.
